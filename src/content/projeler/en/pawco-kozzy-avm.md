For PAWCO Super Pet Store's branch at Kozzy shopping centre we produced and installed an illuminated channel letter sign in 5 mm acrylic across the shop front. The bone-shaped logo was built as an illuminated element in the brand's orange and purple.

Along the shopping centre corridor, the lightbox panels carry PAWCO's campaign artwork and its pet daycare messaging, printed at large format. The entrance glazing has bone-shaped opening-hours film, and the information panels beside the door — "Why ozone?", the payment point and the seven-stage check point — were produced as printed vinyl on PVC board.

Shelf headers and in-store wayfinding were completed in the brand's colours and typography. We ran the whole process from design to installation as part of our [retail branding](/en/services/retail-branding/) service.
