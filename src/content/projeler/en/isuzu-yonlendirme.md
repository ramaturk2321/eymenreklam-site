We designed, produced and installed the wayfinding sign system for the Anadolu Isuzu factory site in Şekerpınar, Çayırova (Kocaeli). The bus and truck plants, logistics areas, R&D centre and academy sit in separate buildings, so signs were placed at every junction on the site to get visitors, suppliers and new staff to the right building first time.

The system uses three sign types: a double-sided, multi-line totem at the main roundabout; post-mounted direction signs at road splits; and single-line building ID signs at entrances. All signs share one design — white text on a dark panel with a red arrow band — matched to the RAL equivalents of Anadolu Isuzu's corporate colours, with production starting only after design approval.

Before installation, staff and vehicle health-and-safety documents were submitted and approved through the factory's site-access system, and the work was carried out without disrupting production. We have since also produced warning signs, waste-area signs, model-logo plates and posters for the same factory.
