We took on the façade signage and cladding for the new Istanbul branches of the Tarım Kredi cooperative supermarket chain (KOOP). At the Maltepe, Bağcılar, Kadıköy, Esenler and Fındıklı branches, green composite panel cladding runs the length of the façade, carrying the brand's white illuminated channel letter "KOOP" logo and green cross.

On corner sites the composite cladding wraps both elevations without a break, and the sign dimensions are engineered separately for each branch according to its façade length. A double-sided "Farmers' Market" wayfinding sign on the pavement side, frosted film and a green band on the window glazing, and composite aisle panels inside the store were all installed by the same team.

To keep one look from branch to branch across a chain, production, installation and final inspection were all run from one place. See our [signage service](/en/services/signage/) and [retail branding](/en/services/retail-branding/) pages for façade signage solutions.
