We produced and installed the façade and interior graphics for Tatbak's new store in Gebze as a single package. The store was handed over ready for opening on 18 September 2026.

On the façade, an illuminated green channel letter Tatbak logo sits on a timber-slatted background, with the fascia band and side columns clad in black composite. The corner window carries an illuminated lightbox with baklava imagery, and the entrance doors and window glazing have the green brand logo applied over frosted film. Opening hours and entrance information were applied to the glass as round wood-effect vinyl labels.

Inside, we produced a full-height product graphic for the nuts section, product photography panels above the shelves, brand headers for the Turkish delight and chocolate aisles with an illuminated aisle panel, and a set of information and warning signs. Every graphic was prepared in colours and sizes that sit with the store's white brick and timber finishes.
