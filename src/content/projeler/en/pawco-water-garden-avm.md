We produced and installed every element of the in-store and façade visual identity for PAWCO Super Pet Store's branch at Water Garden shopping centre in Istanbul. The façade carries an illuminated channel letter PAWCO sign, with a set of illuminated pictograms on either side of the entrance explaining the services offered inside.

Inside the store we installed a paw-shaped illuminated ceiling sign, an illuminated arch above the till, neon LED lettering for the grooming and care salon, and a neon line drawing in the washing area. The paw-shaped seating area, designed as a memory wall, was built as an orange paw form on a textured purple panel.

Shelf headers were produced as purple print on black acrylic, information panels as printed vinyl on PVC board, with paw-print floor vinyl and window film completing the scheme — so the brand's orange-and-purple identity holds at every point in the store.
