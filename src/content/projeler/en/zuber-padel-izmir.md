We produced the brand graphics that wrap the courts at Züber Padel Club's İzmir facility. The ZÜBER banners used along the top band of the courts and on the panel at the facility entrance were digitally printed on 450 gsm PVC banner, finished with eyelets and tensioned onto the existing steel structure.

For the court equipment we produced branded 110×20×5 cm net post padding and 200×20×5 cm and 120×20×5 cm door protection padding. A 61×46 cm scoreboard was supplied and mounted at the court entrance.

The installation completes the facility's red-and-white brand identity, set against the blue of the court surface.
